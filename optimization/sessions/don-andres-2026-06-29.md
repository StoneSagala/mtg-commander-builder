# Don Andres, the Renegade — Optimization Session
**Date:** 2026-06-29  
**Format:** Commander (EDH)  
**Identity:** Grixis theft control — cast opponents' spells, generate Treasures with Don, win with their own cards

---

## Session Notes

First game feedback after build:
- Deck was fun, table enjoyed it, almost won, killed two players
- Curve felt too high and too creature-heavy
- Needed more Treasure generation to fuel Don's ramp engine
- Gonti, Lord of Luxury felt underwhelming

Direction confirmed: "control police" style that lets opponents develop then steals/intercepts their best stuff. NOT a lockdown deck — the personality is "I'll take that" not "no." Table should feel like they're seeing their cards do cool things.

Added passive field effects (Dauthi Voidwalker, Opposition Agent, Tergrid) that sit and vacuum up cards. Mnemonic Betrayal as a late-game highlight reel moment — cast everything in every graveyard with Don out = massive Treasure chain.

---

## Changes Made

### OUT
- Gonti, Lord of Luxury — underwhelming, user confirmed
- Mind's Dilation — 7 mana, too passive, waits on opponents to cast
- Blood Money — off theme, just a board wipe
- Thieving Amalgam — 6 mana, doesn't cast opponents' stuff
- River's Rebuke — 6 mana bounce, off theme
- Agent of Treachery — 7 mana, too expensive
- Mob Rule — Tergrid does this better with board wipes
- Captivating Crew — requires 3R to activate on top of its own cost, too slow

### IN
- Dauthi Voidwalker — 2 mana, exiles all spells going to opponents' graveyards, sacrifice to cast one free
- Opposition Agent — intercepts all tutors, you search their library instead
- Tergrid, God of Fright — when opponents sacrifice or discard, you get those permanents; pairs with Blasphemous Act
- Xorn — doubles all Treasure tokens (doubles Don's output)
- Professional Face-Breaker — 3 mana, combat damage makes Treasures + exile/cast from top
- Jeska's Will — 3 mana, massive ramp + exile 3 from opponent's library to cast
- Praetor's Grasp — 3 mana, surgical theft from any opponent's library
- Mnemonic Betrayal — exile all opponents' graveyards and cast them all until EOT
