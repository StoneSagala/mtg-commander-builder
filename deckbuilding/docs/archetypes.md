# MTG Deck Archetypes

## The Core Four

### Aggro
**Goal:** Win before the opponent can establish their game plan. Minimize opponent's decision-making window.

**Typical game plan:**
- T1: 1-drop threat
- T2: 2-drop + attack
- T3: Pump, burn, or another threat
- T4: Lethal (or T3 in fast formats)

**Curve profile:** Spikes at 1-2 CMC. Nothing above 3-4 without a very specific reason.

**Key card roles:**
- 8-12 one-drops (creatures or spells that replace themselves)
- 8-10 two-drops (efficient threats)
- 8-12 burn/pump spells (close the game)
- 18-22 lands (aggressive mana base, prioritize untapped)

**Colors:** Red (Burn, Sligh), White (Weenie), Red/White (Boros), Green/White (Selesnya Tokens), Red/Black (Rakdos)

**Weaknesses:** Sweepers, lifegain, big blockers, running out of cards in hand.

**Anti-aggro sideboard staples:** Fog effects, cheap sweepers (Anger of the Gods), lifegain (Kor Firewalker, Dragon's Claw)

---

### Control
**Goal:** Answer every threat until you establish a lock or win condition. Trade efficiently until the opponent runs out of resources.

**Typical game plan:**
- T1-T3: Answer threats with removal, counters, sweepers
- T4-T5: Deploy card advantage engines
- T6+: Land a win condition from a dominant resource position

**Curve profile:** Low 1-2 drop interaction, peaks at 2-4 for answers, 1-3 expensive threats/win conditions.

**Key card roles:**
- 8-12 counterspells or cheap removal
- 4-6 sweepers / board wipes
- 4-6 card draw / card advantage engines
- 1-3 win conditions (often hard to kill or immediate impact)
- 24-28 lands (control needs consistent mana)

**Colors:** Blue/White (Azorius), Blue/Black (Dimir), Blue/White/Black (Esper)

**Weaknesses:** Uncounterable threats, planeswalkers, resolved permanents that generate ongoing value.

**Anti-control sideboard staples:** Spell pierce, Veil of Summer, threats that generate value when countered.

---

### Midrange
**Goal:** Play the most efficient threats and answers for each point of the game. Win the war of attrition by trading up at every opportunity.

**Typical game plan:**
- T1-T2: Disrupt opponent (hand disruption, cheap removal) OR develop mana
- T3-T4: Deploy above-rate threats
- T5+: Generate card advantage while attacking

**Curve profile:** Bell curve peaking at 3. Some 1-2 drops, heaviest at 3-4, a few 5-6 haymakers.

**Key card roles:**
- 4-8 hand disruption or cheap removal
- 10-14 efficient threats (must trade up vs aggro, trade down vs control)
- 4-6 card advantage engines
- 2-4 top-end finishers

**Colors:** Green/Black (Golgari "The Rock"), Black (Mono-Black), Jund (RBG), Abzan (WBG)

**Weaknesses:** Pure aggro going under, combo going over, control grinding resources.

**The rule of midrange:** Every card should be good in multiple matchups. Narrow hate cards don't belong in the main deck.

---

### Combo
**Goal:** Assemble a specific set of cards that win the game on the spot or create an infinite loop.

**Typical game plan:**
- T1-T3: Dig for combo pieces (cantrips, tutors, draw effects)
- T4-T?: Protect the combo with interaction, then execute

**Curve profile:** Highly variable. Depends entirely on the combo. Fast combos spike low; grindy combos have normal curves with the combo as the payoff.

**Key card roles:**
- 4-8 copies of key combo pieces (or tutors for them)
- 8-12 dig / filtering spells (cantrips, looting)
- 4-8 protection (counterspells, discard, removal for hate pieces)
- The combo itself (usually 2-3 cards that interact)

**Colors:** Any, but Blue is most common (cantrips + counterspells)

**Weaknesses:** Disruption (counterspells, hand disruption, removal), hate cards (graveyard exile, enchantment removal, Stony Silence)

## Sub-Archetypes & Hybrids

### Tempo
A hybrid of aggro and control. Play efficient threats and disrupt the opponent's development long enough for threats to close the game.

- Threats that also answer things (e.g., Snapcaster Mage, Brazen Borrower)
- Cheap, temporary interaction (Mana Leak, Spell Pierce, unsustainable but buy time)
- Colors: Blue/Red (Izzet), Blue/Green (Simic)

### Ramp
A variant of midrange/combo that uses mana acceleration to deploy threats or spells ahead of curve.

- Ramp package: 8-12 ramp cards (Cultivate, Rampant Growth, dorks)
- Big threats at 5-8 CMC that justify the ramp investment
- Colors: Green-based (often splashing a second or third color)

### Tokens
An aggro/midrange hybrid that creates many small threats and wins through go-wide strategies.

- Token generators (Lingering Souls, Raise the Alarm, Goblin Rabblemaster)
- Anthem effects (Intangible Virtue, Glorious Anthem)
- Usually White or Green/White

### Burn
Pure aggro focused on dealing damage directly rather than combat.

- 20+ spells that deal damage to the face
- Game plan ends at 20 damage, not on a creature dying
- Red primarily

### Stompy
A creature-heavy midrange variant without much interaction. Just big green creatures.

- Efficient 2-4 drop creatures with high stats
- Minimal non-creature spells
- Green primarily

## Archetype Interaction Matrix

| Your Archetype | vs. Aggro | vs. Control | vs. Combo | vs. Midrange |
|----------------|-----------|-------------|-----------|--------------|
| **Aggro** | Race | Generally favored | Race to kill before combo | Slightly disadvantaged (blockers) |
| **Control** | Defensive sweepers needed | Grinds out | Need specific hate | Grinds out |
| **Combo** | Race to go off | Need protection | Race or disrupt | Disruption matters most |
| **Midrange** | Removal and blockers | Threats that beat counters | Disruption package | Quality of cards |

## Commander-Specific Archetypes

### Voltron
Win by attacking with a single, heavily-buffed commander. Load up on equipment and auras.
- Commander selection: cheap, high-impact commander with innate keywords (evasion, protection)
- Equipment: Sword cycle, Hammer of Nazahn, Lightning Greaves
- Auras: Eldrazi Conscription, Shielded by Faith

### Group Hug
Enable everyone at the table with symmetrical effects while advancing your own win condition subtly.
- Mass card draw, extra land drops for everyone
- Political tools
- Win condition is usually something nobody expects

### Stax / Prison
Slow down opponents with asymmetrical resource denial.
- Mana denial: Winter Orb, Tangle Wire, Smokestack
- Card draw denial: Notion Thief, Hullbreacher
- Be aware: heavy stax creates negative play experiences

### Spellslinger
Win through casting many instants and sorceries and using payoffs that care about the spell count.
- Guttersnipe, Thousand-Year Storm, Arclight Phoenix style payoffs
- Blue/Red (Izzet) most common

### Tribal
Win by leveraging creature type synergies.
- Changelings are universal tribe members
- Lord effects (+1/+1 to all creatures of type X)
- Examples: Elves, Goblins, Merfolk, Zombies, Dragons, Slivers
