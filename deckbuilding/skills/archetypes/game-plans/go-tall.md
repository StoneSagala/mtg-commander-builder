# Game Plan: Go Tall

Win through quality over quantity. Build one creature (usually the commander) into an overwhelming threat, then push it through for lethal damage. Every card in the deck either makes that creature bigger, harder to remove, or able to attack freely.

---

## How the Game Plays Out

**Early (turns 1–3):** Ramp and set up. Get the commander into play and establish protection.

**Mid (turns 4–6):** Stack buffs. Equip, enchant, or trigger effects that grow the threat. The commander should represent a 3-hit clock at minimum by turn 5.

**Late (turn 5+):** Clear a path and swing. Evasion and removal clear blockers; the threat closes in 2–3 hits.

---

## What the Deck Needs

- **Evasion** — the single most important keyword; a 20-power creature that can't connect does nothing; flying, trample, menace, or unblockable
- **Protection** — the threat is the deck; losing it to removal sets back 3+ turns; Lightning Greaves, Swiftfoot Boots mandatory
- **Power scaling** — how does the threat get to lethal quickly? Equipment (Colossus Hammer), auras (Eldrazi Conscription), counters (+1/+1 doublers), or natural growth
- **Haste** — the best protection is never giving opponents priority before you swing
- **Evasion-granting equipment** — Trailblazer's Boots, Prowler's Helm, Whispersilk Cloak pull double duty (protection + evasion)

---

## Interaction Requirements

- **Proactive** — Go Tall is the most proactive game plan; interaction slots are minimal
- Priority: protect your threat > interact with opponents
- Include 1–2 answers for specific hate cards (Propaganda, Ghostly Prison, Maze of Ith)
- Board wipes from opponents are your worst nightmare; Boros Charm, Indestructibility

---

## Common Vulnerabilities

- Tuck effects (Chaos Warp, Oblation) exile or shuffle the commander; no recursion from graveyard
- Pillow fort and damage prevention shut down combat entirely
- "Destroy all creatures" board wipes reset everything
- Go Tall is linear — opponents know the plan and can prepare hate
