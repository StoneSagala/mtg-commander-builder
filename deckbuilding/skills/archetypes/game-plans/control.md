# Game Plan: Control

Answer threats as they appear. Outlast opponents through superior card advantage and reactive interaction, then win cleanly from a position of dominance.

---

## How the Game Plays Out

**Early:** Draw cards and hold up mana. Answer the fastest threat at the table.

**Mid:** Establish card advantage engines (Rhystic Study, Necropotence). Keep the board manageable with targeted removal and sweepers. Let opponents exhaust each other.

**Late:** Deploy the win condition from a position where opponents are tapped out or depleted.

---

## What the Deck Needs

- **Card draw engines** — Control lives on card advantage; need 3–4 passive draw sources (Rhystic Study, Phyrexian Arena, Sylvan Library, Mystic Remora)
- **Counterspells** — 4–6 counterspells at minimum; prioritize flexibility (Arcane Denial, Swan Song, Counterspell)
- **Targeted removal** — answer specific threats without losing tempo (Swords to Plowshares, Generous Gift, Beast Within)
- **Board wipes** — 2–3 sweepers for when boards get ahead of spot removal (Wrath of God, Cyclonic Rift, Toxic Deluge)
- **A win condition that needs one window** — win cons that require sustained board presence are risky; prefer instant-speed or protected finishers (combo, Approach of the Second Sun)

---

## Mana Curve Philosophy

Control runs lean on the curve early and holds up mana. Every 2–3 mana should represent either an answer or a draw spell. Spending all mana proactively means nothing to counter on opponents' turns.

**Ideal pattern:** Cast draw spell on your turn, hold up counterspell on opponent's turn.

---

## Interaction Requirements

- **Reactive** — this is the definition of a reactive game plan
- 10–12 interaction slots (counterspells + removal + sweepers)
- Flash speed permanents (Glen Elendra Archmage, Vendilion Clique) allow interaction at instant speed without sacrificing tempo

---

## Common Vulnerabilities

- Too much removal and not enough win conditions — the deck draws well but never wins
- Mana-hungry — holding up interaction and advancing board state simultaneously requires significant mana
- Vulnerable to Boseiju, Who Endures and uncounterable spells
- Group dynamics — control draws attention early; table may coordinate against it

---

## Bracket Notes

- **Brackets 1–2:** Soft control — reactive removal, minimal counterspells (pods dislike heavy counters at low brackets)
- **Bracket 3:** 4–6 counterspells; Cyclonic Rift as premier sweeper
- **Brackets 4–5:** Full permission suite; Force of Will, Fierce Guardianship, Mana Drain

---

## Pairs Well With

- **Engines:** Any draw engine — control needs cards above all else
- **Win Cons:** Combo (execute from a clear board), Alternate Win Cons (Approach of the Second Sun), Commander Damage (one protected threat)
