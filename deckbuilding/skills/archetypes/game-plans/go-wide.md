# Game Plan: Go Wide

Win through quantity. Flood the board with creatures or tokens, then close with a single overrun effect or by overwhelming blockers through sheer numbers.

---

## How the Game Plays Out

**Early (turns 1–3):** Establish a token generator or creature engine. The deck should produce 2–4 creatures per turn by midgame.

**Mid (turns 4–6):** Scale the board. Anthem effects, sacrifice payoffs, or combat tricks come online. Opponents either interact or fall behind on board presence.

**Late (turn 6+):** One overrun effect closes — Craterhoof Behemoth, Overwhelming Stampede, Triumph of the Hordes, or a single large combat swing with sufficient power on board.

---

## What the Deck Needs

- **Consistent token production** — at least 4–6 cards that generate multiple tokens per turn or over time
- **An overrun/finisher** — the win button that turns a flood of 1/1s into lethal damage; most Go Wide decks need 2 of these
- **Haste enablers** — tokens that can't attack the turn they arrive slow the strategy significantly; Swiftfoot Boots, Mass Hysteria, Fires of Yavimaya
- **Evasion on the swarm** — trample or flying grant (Beastmaster Ascension, Akroma's Memorial, Eldrazi Monument)
- **Protection from wipes** — the worst outcome for Go Wide; Heroic Intervention, Teferi's Protection, or a sacrifice outlet to preempt wipes

---

## Interaction Requirements

- **Proactive to Balanced** — Go Wide doesn't need heavy interaction; it races to end the game
- Minimum 1–2 board wipes of your own as reset buttons when opponents get ahead
- Spot removal is secondary; prioritize cards that protect your board over removal

---

## Common Vulnerabilities

- Single board wipe can undo 3–4 turns of development
- Individual token quality is low — a single deathtouch blocker can stall the whole army
- Slow against stax/control that answers every token generator

- **Engines:** Tokens (primary), Aristocrats (sacrifice payoffs on the swarm), Tribal (lord effects)
- **Win Cons:** Life Depletion (combat), Poison (Triumph of the Hordes line)
