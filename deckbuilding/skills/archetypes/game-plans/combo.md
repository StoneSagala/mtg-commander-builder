# Game Plan: Combo

Win by assembling a specific set of 2–4 cards that create a game-ending loop or instant win. Everything in the deck either finds pieces, protects the combo, or generates mana to execute it.

---

## How the Game Plays Out

**Early:** Ramp and draw. Find combo pieces while staying alive.

**Mid:** Assemble pieces. Tutors, card draw, and library manipulation accelerate assembly.

**Win turn:** Execute the combo with protection held up (counterspells, silence effects, Veil of Summer). Announce the loop; demonstrate it fully to table.

---

## The Combo Pieces

Lock these in before building anything else. A combo deck's non-negotiables are:

1. **The primary combo** — all pieces listed explicitly (e.g., Thassa's Oracle + Demonic Consultation)
2. **Redundant paths** — a second combo that wins from a similar position (e.g., Thoracle + Tainted Pact as backup)
3. **Tutors to find pieces** — scaled to bracket (Demonic Tutor at bracket 4+, Diabolic Tutor at bracket 3)

**If you cannot identify at least 2 distinct combo paths, the deck is not ready to build.**

---

## What the Deck Needs

- **The combo pieces themselves** — lock these in first, everything else supports them
- **Redundant tutors** — at least 3 tutor effects at bracket 3; 5+ at bracket 4
- **Card draw to find pieces** — Rhystic Study, Mystic Remora, Necropotence at higher brackets
- **Interaction on the win turn** — Force of Will, Swan Song, Veil of Summer; opponents WILL have answers
- **Fast mana** at bracket 4+ — Mana Vault, Mana Crypt, Chrome Mox to execute before opponents establish

---

## Interaction Requirements

- **Reactive to Balanced** — need enough interaction to survive until combo turn
- **Counterspells** over spot removal — protect the combo rather than answer the board
- **Stack interaction specifically** — the combo resolves on the stack; boardstates matter less

---

## Bracket Restrictions (Critical)

| Bracket | Combo Policy |
|---------|-------------|
| 1 | No infinite combos |
| 2 | No infinite combos |
| 3 | Late-game combos only (not consistently assembling before turn 6) |
| 4–5 | All combos; early game combos expected |

**Early-game combo = can consistently win before turn 6.**

---

## Common Vulnerabilities

- Combo pieces in hand telegraphed by drawing many cards
- Stax pieces (Rule of Law, Eidolon of Rhetoric) prevent the win turn
- Graveyard hate disrupts reanimation-combo lines
- Single counterspell on the win turn can end the game in your loss

---

## Pairs Well With

- **Engines:** Any — combo is a game plan, not an engine
- **Win Cons:** All — the combo determines the win con (drain loop = Life Depletion, Thoracle = Alternate Win Con, etc.)
