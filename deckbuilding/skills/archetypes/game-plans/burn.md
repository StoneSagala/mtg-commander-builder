# Game Plan: Burn / Non-Combat Damage

Deal damage to opponents through spells, triggered abilities, and permanents rather than combat. The board state is secondary — damage sources are the primary resource.

---

## How the Game Plays Out

**Early:** Establish passive damage sources — permanents that deal damage whenever something happens (creatures entering, spells cast, etc.).

**Mid:** Stack multiple damage sources. Each trigger deals 1–3 damage; with 3–4 sources in play, every action deals 6–12 damage to each opponent.

**Late:** One large spell or mass ETB event closes the game. Purphoros with 20 tokens ETBing simultaneously = 40 damage to each opponent.

---

## The Damage Sources

**ETB triggers:** Purphoros, God of the Forge; Impact Tremors; Warstorm Surge; Stalactite Stalker
**Spell triggers:** Guttersnipe; Firebrand Archer; Electrostatic Field; Kessler's Flamekeeper
**Mana dumps:** Torment of Hailfire; Exsanguinate; Comet Storm; Banefire
**Direct payoffs:** Aetherflux Reservoir (storm); Grapeshot; Tendrils of Agony

---

## What the Deck Needs

- **Passive damage engines** (2–4) — permanents that trigger without additional cost
- **Density trigger** — a way to trigger many times in one turn (mass ETB event, casting 10+ spells in a storm turn)
- **Mana ramp** — Torment of Hailfire scales with mana; Aetherflux Reservoir rewards high spell counts
- **Protection for damage sources** — opponents will prioritize removing Purphoros immediately
- **Card draw** — need to find triggers and maintain fuel

---

## Interaction Requirements

- **Proactive** — this game plan doesn't interact; it races to deal lethal damage
- Minimum interaction: 2–3 removal pieces for hate cards (Leyline of Punishment for lifegain; Torpor Orb removal)
- Counter-based protection for key permanents is more valuable than removal

---

## Common Vulnerabilities

- Leyline of Sanctity / Sphere of Safety — prevent being targeted by damage sources
- Torpor Orb — shuts off ETB triggers entirely; must answer immediately
- Lifegain — opponents gaining 10+ life negates several turns of damage; run lifegain hate
- Damage prevention effects (Solemnity + Phyrexian Unlife)
