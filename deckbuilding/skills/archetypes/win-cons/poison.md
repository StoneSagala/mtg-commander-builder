# Win Condition: Poison Counters

Deal 10 poison counters to an opponent using infect, toxic, or proliferate. Poison counters are tracked separately from life total — an opponent at 40 life with 10 poison counters loses immediately.

---

## How It Works

Three ways to accumulate poison counters:

**Infect** — creatures with infect deal damage as poison counters to players (and -1/-1 counters to creatures). A 3-power infect creature deals 3 poison in one hit.

**Toxic** — creatures with toxic deal regular combat damage AND add poison equal to the toxic value. Does not replace damage.

**Proliferate** — add one counter of each type already present. One poison counter + proliferate = progress without attacking.

---

## What the Deck Needs

- **Evasion on infect creatures** — a blocked infect creature does nothing useful; flying, trample, and unblockable are essential
- **Power boost** — infect math: 10 damage = win; a 2-power infect creature needs 5 unblocked hits; a 10-power infect creature wins in one hit with a pump spell
- **Protection** — losing the infect creature mid-combat to a Fog or instant-speed removal loses the entire turn; Lightning Greaves mandatory
- **Pump spells** — one-shot potential with Scale Up, Berserk, Become Immense, Hatred
- **Proliferate** (optional) — extends the win condition beyond combat; Grateful Apparition, Flux Channeler, Karn's Bastion

## Redundancy Target

**2–3 infect creatures** that can each close independently with pump support. Proliferate as a backup accumulation engine.

---

## Vulnerabilities

- Removal mid-combat after blocks are declared but before damage
- -1/-1 counters and board wipes reset infect creatures
- Lifelink does NOT prevent poison — opponents cannot heal poison damage
- Single target: can only kill one opponent per attack unless using Triumph of the Hordes (all creatures gain infect until end of turn)

---

## Bracket Notes

- **Brackets 1–2:** Infect is typically unwelcome socially; flag with pod before including
- **Bracket 3:** Viable with restraint; avoid Skithiryx, the Blight Dragon at this level
- **Bracket 4–5:** Fastest kill con in Commander; Phyrexian Crusader, Skithiryx, Blightsteel Colossus

---

## Pairs Well With

- **Engines:** Counters/Proliferate (extend progress without attacking), Blink-ETB (if infect creatures have ETBs)
- **Game Plans:** Go Tall (one big infect hit), Combo (Triumph of the Hordes as a win button)
