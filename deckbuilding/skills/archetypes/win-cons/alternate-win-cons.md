# Win Condition: Alternate Win Condition Cards

Win by meeting a specific card condition rather than reducing life, dealing commander damage, milling, or poisoning. These are "you win the game" text triggers that bypass all normal win conditions.

---

## The Cards

### Thassa's Oracle
**Condition:** Have devotion to blue ≥ cards in library (or empty library) when ETB resolves.
**Path:** Self-mill + blue devotion. Hermit Druid, Mesmeric Orb, or Laboratory Maniac backup.
**Interaction point:** Opponents can respond to the ETB trigger. Needs protection (Veil of Summer, Swan Song held up). Cannot be responded to if the trigger is on the stack and opponent has no interaction.
**Note:** Counterspell after Oracle enters but before trigger resolves does NOT stop it — the trigger is already on the stack.

### Felidar Sovereign
**Condition:** Have 40+ life at the beginning of your upkeep.
**Path:** Life gain engine. Oloro, Ageless Ascetic; Aetherflux Reservoir; Soul Warden chains.
**Interaction point:** Opponents have an entire round to remove it before your next upkeep. Needs haste or instant-speed protection.
**Note:** This is one of the easiest alt win cons to disrupt — build lifegain plan independently of the win con.

### Approach of the Second Sun
**Condition:** Cast it a second time in the same game, having cast it at least 7 cards ago.
**Path:** Card draw + tutors to find it, then survive long enough to cast twice. Control game plan preferred.
**Note:** Opponents can counter the second cast. Need protection mana or a counterspell backup.

### Maze's End
**Condition:** Control 10 or more differently-named Gate lands and activate the ability.
**Path:** Gate tribal. Circuitous Route, Gatecreeper Vine, Gate Colossus as support.
**Note:** Very telegraphed. Opponents will destroy Maze's End on sight. Run 2–3 ways to recur it (Crucible of Worlds, Ramunap Excavator).

### Revel in Riches
**Condition:** Control 10 or more Treasure tokens at the beginning of your upkeep.
**Path:** Kill opponent creatures to create Treasures (whenever an opponent's creature dies). Feeds into artifact theme naturally.
**Note:** Requires opponents to have creatures dying. Include removal that kills their creatures, not just your own sacrifice effects.

### Mechanized Production
**Condition:** Control 8 or more artifacts sharing a name at the beginning of your upkeep.
**Path:** Token artifact generation. Myr tokens, Thopter tokens, Treasure tokens. Brudiclad, Telchor Engineer is a natural commander for this.
**Note:** Opponents must let you survive an upkeep with 8 identical artifacts. Needs protection or flash speed setup.

### Helix Pinnacle
**Condition:** Have 100 counters on Helix Pinnacle at the beginning of your upkeep.
**Path:** Massive mana generation. Selvala, Heart of the Wilds; Nyxbloom Ancient; Gaea's Cradle. Green ramp engine required.
**Note:** Long clock — requires significant mana investment over several turns. Best in untap-heavy or big mana builds.

### Triskaidekaphobe / Triskaidekaphile
**Condition:** Have exactly 13 cards in hand at the beginning of your upkeep.
**Path:** Hand size manipulation. Reliquary Tower, Thought Vessel. Windfall, Wheel of Fortune to reset hand size.
**Note:** Fragile — any discard effect or draw trigger can knock you off 13. Build hand protection (Teferi's Protection, City of Solitude).

## Universal Requirements for Alt Win Cons

1. **The condition must be achievable reliably** — build the entire deck around enabling it, not as a one-of "surprise"
2. **Protection at the moment of trigger** — almost all alt win cons require surviving one opponent's upkeep or passing priority; have interaction held up
3. **A backup win condition** — if the alt win con gets exiled or countered, the deck needs another path; don't make it the only plan
4. **Redundancy** — run 1–2 tutor effects to find the win con card

## Pairs Well With

- **Engines:** Artifacts (Revel in Riches, Mechanized Production), Enchantress (Approach, Helix Pinnacle), Lands (Maze's End), Graveyard (Thassa's Oracle self-mill path)
- **Game Plans:** Control (protect the condition), Combo (instant-speed enablement)
