# Win Condition: Commander Damage

Deal 21 combat damage to a single opponent using the commander. Each player tracks commander damage from each commander separately — 21 from yours eliminates that player regardless of their life total.

---

## How It Works

The commander must deal combat damage to a player. Abilities that deal damage (ping effects) do not count — it must be combat damage specifically.

21 damage from a 5-power commander = 5 hits minimum. The goal is to reduce that to 3–4 swings using power buffs, double strike, or trample.

---

## What the Deck Needs

- **Power boost** — commander needs to be threatening (7+ power is the common benchmark for closing quickly)
- **Evasion** — flying, trample, menace, or unblockable; without it, opponents chump block indefinitely
- **Haste** — especially important; a commander that can't attack the turn it enters gives opponents a full turn to find removal
- **Protection** — Lightning Greaves, Swiftfoot Boots, Heroic Intervention; a single removal spell on swing turn loses significant tempo
- **Double strike** — the highest-value keyword for this win con; cuts required hits in half

---

## Kill Threshold Reference

| Commander Power | Hits Needed (no double strike) | Hits w/ Double Strike |
|----------------|-------------------------------|----------------------|
| 5 | 5 | 3 |
| 7 | 3 | 2 |
| 10 | 3 | 2 |
| 14+ | 2 | 1 |

Infect modifiers (if relevant): commander damage from infect creatures uses poison counters, not the 21-damage rule. Different win condition.

---

## Redundancy

Commander damage is a single-path win con — the commander IS the win condition. This requires:
- High commander dependency (Central role in brief)
- Multiple protection pieces (3+ minimum)
- A backup plan if commander is tucked or exiled (recast route vs. alternate win con)

---

## Vulnerabilities

- Tuck effects (exile from game, shuffle into library) — far worse than destroy
- Damage prevention (Fog, Teferi's Protection, Ghostly Prison)
- Chump block armies — evasion is mandatory, not optional
- High commander tax after repeated removal
