# Win Condition: Library Depletion (Mill)

Win by causing an opponent to draw from an empty library, or by directly exiling all cards from their library. An opponent with no cards in library loses the game when they need to draw.

---

## How It Works

Two approaches:

**Active mill** — directly put cards from opponent's libraries into their graveyards. Traumatize, Maddening Cacophony, Glimpse the Unthinkable, Tasha's Hideous Laughter.

**Forced draw** — make opponents draw more cards than their library holds. Forced draw sources: Seizan Perverter of Truth, Howling Mine + Teferi's Puzzle Box loops, Words of Waste.

In Commander, each opponent has ~60 card libraries. Milling all three simultaneously requires either massive single-target hits or recursive loops.

---

## What the Deck Needs

- **High mill rate** — individual cards that mill 5–10 cards are not enough at Commander scale; need recursive mills or mass mill events
- **Graveyard protection for opponents** — shuffle effects (Elixir of Immortality, Kozilek reanimation trigger) reset mill progress; run Bojuka Bog, Leyline of the Void, or Tasha's Hideous Laughter (exiles instead of mills)
- **Self-protection** — mill decks are slow and draw early aggro; need pillow fort or interaction to survive
- **A kill reset counter** — if one opponent shuffles their graveyard back, you need enough mill in reserve to redo it

---

## Redundancy Target

**2–3 high-volume mill sources** that each threaten to finish a player alone. Supplement with recursive low-cost mills.

---

## Vulnerabilities

- Elixir of Immortality / Kozilek, Butcher of Truth — reset mill progress entirely; run graveyard exile (not just removal)
- Thassa's Oracle / Laboratory Maniac — opponents can win from their own empty library before you can
- Mill is slow in multiplayer; you need to answer multiple opponents' libraries
- Opponents may be able to thin their deck or draw from empty (Abundance, Obstinate Familiar)

## Pairs Well With

- **Engines:** Spellslinger (high spell count triggers mill), Artifacts (Mesmeric Orb loops), Graveyard (self-mill decks can mill opponents as secondary win)
- **Game Plans:** Control (survive long enough to mill out), Combo (infinite mill loop)
