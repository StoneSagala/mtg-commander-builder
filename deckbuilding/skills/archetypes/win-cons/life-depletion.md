# Win Condition: Life Depletion

Opponents start at 40 life. Win by reducing all opponents to 0 through any combination of combat damage, non-combat damage, and life drain.

---

## How It Works

Three distinct paths — most Life Depletion decks use more than one:

**Combat damage** — creatures deal damage in combat. Scale via power buffs, evasion, or sheer quantity (Go Wide / Go Tall game plans).

**Burn / direct damage** — spells and permanents deal damage without combat. Purphoros, God of the Forge; Impact Tremors; Torment of Hailfire; Comet Storm.

**Life drain** — opponents lose life while you may gain it. Blood Artist; Zulaport Cutthroat; Syr Konrad, the Grim; Exquisite Blood loops.

---

## What the Deck Needs

- A reliable damage source that scales (one ping per turn doesn't close games at 40 life)
- Reach — a way to deal the final 5–10 damage when opponents stabilize
- Speed calibrated to bracket: casual pods go to turn 8–10; optimized pods may need to close turn 5–7

---

## Redundancy Target

Run **2–3 distinct damage paths**. If your only damage source is creatures and the board gets wiped, you have no way to close. Mix at least two of: combat, drain, burn.

---

## Vulnerabilities

- Lifegain: opponents gaining 10+ life extends the game significantly — include at least 1 lifegain hate piece (Erebos, God of the Dead; Leyline of Punishment; Quakebringer)
- Board wipes reset combat-only paths
- Pillow fort and damage prevention (Fog effects, Ghostly Prison) stop combat
