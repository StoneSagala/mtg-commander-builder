# Engine: Tokens

Create many small creatures at low cost, then scale them through anthem effects, sacrifice payoffs, or sheer numbers. The engine is self-sustaining: token generators create the army, payoffs reward the army's existence.

---

## How Value Is Generated

**Mass token generators** — one card creates many tokens simultaneously or over time; Doubling Season doubles the output of every generator.

**Anthem effects** — pump the whole army; cost-efficient because one card buffs all tokens.

**Sacrifice payoffs** — tokens are expendable; sac outlets turn the army into mana, cards, or damage.

**Token doublers** — Anointed Procession, Parallel Lives, Mondrak, Glory Dominus; exponential scaling from the same generators.

---

## Core Card Types Required

- **Token generators (6–10)** — cards that reliably create 2+ tokens per turn or per cast; prioritize repeatable generators over one-time effects
- **Token doublers (1–3)** — Anointed Procession, Parallel Lives; not mandatory but dramatically accelerate the plan
- **Anthem effects (3–4)** — Intangible Virtue, Beastmaster Ascension, Craterhoof Behemoth; army quality matters at the end
- **Haste enablers** — new tokens that can't attack stall the engine; Swiftfoot Boots, Fires of Yavimaya, Rhythm of the Wild
- **Sacrifice outlets** — preempt board wipes; convert tokens into value before they die

## Token Types Matter

| Token Type | Natural Synergy |
|-----------|----------------|
| Creatures | Combat, sacrifice, tribal |
| Treasure | Mana, artifact payoffs |
| Food | Lifegain, sacrifice |
| Clue/Map | Card draw |

Building primarily around creature tokens is most common. Mixed token types require mixed payoffs.

---

## Anti-Synergy — Avoid

- Cards that care about individual creature quality (tokens are weak individually)
- Strategies that require specific named creatures on board
- Decks that need their own graveyard — tokens dying fills it with anonymous tokens, not useful cards
