# Engine: Artifacts

Generate value through artifact synergies — cost reduction, ETB triggers, artifact count payoffs, and fast mana. Artifacts are resilient to sorcery-speed removal and provide the highest density of mana acceleration in the format.

---

## How Value Is Generated

**Fast mana** — Sol Ring, Mana Vault and Grim Monolith accelerate development by 2–4 turns. Artifact decks play their key pieces far ahead of curve.

**Cost reduction** — Foundry Inspector, Etherium Sculptor, Jhoira's Familiar reduce artifacts to near-free; enables chaining multiple artifacts per turn.

**ETB payoffs** — Marionette Master, Reckless Fireweaver, Disciple of the Vault deal damage on artifact ETBs. Panharmonicon doubles ETBs.

**Artifact count payoffs** — Darksteel Juggernaut, Cyberdrive Awakener, Brudiclad all care about artifact count hitting thresholds.

**Treasure/Gold** — treasure token generators (Smothering Tithe and Pitiless Plunderer) are a sub-engine that bridges to other strategies.

---

## Core Card Types Required

- **Mana rocks** — beyond Sol Ring/Signet; the engine needs 8–12 rocks at all CMC ranges
- **Artifact draw** — Thoughtcast, Sai Master Thopterist, Vedalken Archmage; keep hand full while deploying
- **A payoff that scales with count** — at least 1–2 cards that reward having many artifacts in play
- **Recursion** — Goblin Welder, Daretti Scrap Savant, Scrap Trawler; artifacts die to removal, need recursion

## Anti-Synergy — Avoid

- Artifact hate pieces that hurt your own engine (Null Rod, Stony Silence) — obvious but sometimes included accidentally
- Heavy enchantment or creature focus that dilutes artifact count below payoff thresholds
- Non-artifact win conditions that don't interact with the artifact pile

## Common Pairings

- **Game Plans:** Combo (fast mana enables early combo), Stax (artifact stax breaks artifact parity), Go Wide (Thopter/Myr tokens)
- **Win Cons:** Life Depletion (ETB damage), Alternate Win Cons (Revel in Riches, Mechanized Production), Commander Damage (equipment-based Voltron)
