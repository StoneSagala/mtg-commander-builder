# Engine: Theft / Chaos

Generate value from opponents' resources — their cards, creatures, and spells — or by introducing randomness that disrupts predictable game states. The engine's card pool is whatever opponents have, making it scale with what's at the table.

---

## How Value Is Generated

**Theft** — take and use opponents' permanents and spells; Bribery, Expropriate, Agent of Treachery, Chaos Warp (their best permanent becomes random). Value scales with opponents' decks.

**Spell theft** — counter and recast, or copy opponent's spells; Spelljack, Dack Fayden, Izzet Guildmage (copy spells); steal the most powerful spell at the table.

**Chaos** — Possibility Storm, Warp World, Scrambleverse; randomize game state; especially effective when opponents have degenerate setups to disrupt.

**Cast without paying mana costs** — Gonti, Lord of Luxury; Knowledge Pool; cascade; cast opponents' exiled cards for free, bypassing color restrictions.

---

## Core Card Types Required

- **High-value theft targets** — theft is only as good as what's at the table; needs a range of targets (creatures, artifacts, instants/sorceries)
- **Haste enablers** — stolen creatures must attack the turn they're stolen or opponents may get them back; Lightning Greaves, Mass Hysteria, Swiftfoot Boots
- **Cast-from-exile effects** — Gonti, Etali Primal Storm, Ragavan Nimble Pilferer; exile cards and cast them without mana cost restrictions
- **Chaos pieces** (if chaos-focused) — Possibility Storm, Hive Mind, Knowledge Pool; need enough chaos pieces that the disruption is meaningful, not incidental

---

## Two Modes

**Theft (focused):** Take specific high-value permanents or spells. Rewards knowledge of opponents' decks. Strong in known metas.

**Chaos (focused):** Randomize and disrupt. Rewards positioning in chaotic game states. Strong against degenerate combos and linear strategies.

Most theft/chaos decks blend both — use theft for precision, chaos for general disruption.

---

## Anti-Synergy — Avoid

- Cards that depend on your own card pool being consistent (theft decks are inherently inconsistent)
- Graveyard synergies requiring YOUR cards in graveyard (opponents' cards don't fuel your yard)
- Strategies that need specific named cards in play (stolen cards are random)

---

## Bracket Scaling

- **Brackets 1–2:** Light theft as a fun element; Gonti, Bribery as highlight cards
- **Bracket 3:** Etali, Primal Storm as a game-swinging threat; Possibility Storm as disruption
- **Brackets 4–5:** Ragavan for early acceleration; Dack Fayden + artifact theft; Hive Mind + Pact combos

---

## Common Pairings

- **Game Plans:** Chaos/Politics (natural overlap), Control (answers opponents, then steals their answers), Combo (Knowledge Pool + Rule of Law = lock)
- **Win Cons:** Life Depletion (stolen creatures for combat), Commander Damage (stolen finishers), Alternate Win Cons (Chaos Warp surprise)
