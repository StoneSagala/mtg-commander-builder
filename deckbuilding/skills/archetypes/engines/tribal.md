# Engine: Tribal

Build around a shared creature type. Critical mass of tribe members unlocks lord effects, tribal card draw, and synergy pieces that make the entire army stronger than the sum of its parts.

---

## How Value Is Generated

**Lords** — creatures that give +1/+1 (or more) to all creatures of the same type; stack multiple lords to turn a 1/1 into a 4/4 without spending additional resources.

**Tribal card draw** — Kindred Discovery, Distant Melody, Vanquisher's Banner; draw from casting tribe members or having them enter play.

**Tribal effects** — Cavern of Souls (uncounterable), Coat of Arms (exponential power), Door of Destinies (growing anthem), Eidolon of Rhetoric (type-specific).

**Tribe-specific synergies** — Zombies recur, Elves generate mana, Goblins deal damage, Vampires drain life, Dragons have natural evasion and high power.

---

## Core Card Types Required

- **Tribe members: 20+ minimum** — below 20, the tribal synergies don't fire reliably; above 30 creates a coherent army
- **Lords (3–5)** — the primary power amplifier; stack them
- **Tribal card draw (2–3)** — the engine runs out of gas quickly without draw
- **Tribal tutor** — Morophon, the Boundless; Arcane Savant; Muddle the Mixture (for specific tribes)
- **Cavern of Souls** — protection from counterspells, available to every tribe

---

## Anti-Synergy — Avoid

- Non-tribe creatures that dilute below 20-member critical mass
- Generic goodstuff creatures that don't share the type
- Strategies that rely on individual card quality rather than synergy density

## Common Pairings

- **Game Plans:** Go Wide (flood board with tribe members), Combo (tribe-specific infinite loops), Aggro (tribal buffs + haste)
- **Win Cons:** Life Depletion (combat with buffed tribe), Commander Damage (tribe buffs boost commander)
