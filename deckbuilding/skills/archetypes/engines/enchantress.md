# Engine: Enchantress

Draw cards by casting and controlling enchantments. Each enchantment played replaces itself (or better), building a self-sustaining loop of card advantage and board presence.

---

## How Value Is Generated

Enchantress payoffs trigger when enchantments are cast or enter: Enchantress's Presence, Sythis, Harvest's Hand, Eidolon of Blossoms, Setessan Champion. Each enchantment cast draws 1+ cards, meaning the engine is self-funding.

Secondary value: enchantments as permanent protection (Sphere of Safety, Privileged Position), board control (Grasp of Fate, Imprisoned in the Moon), or lock pieces (Stasis, Humility).

---

## Core Card Types Required

- **Enchantress draw payoffs** — 3–5 cards that draw on enchantment cast/ETB (required; without these the engine doesn't function)
- **Low-cost enchantments** — CMC 1–2 enchantments to trigger payoffs cheaply; cantrip enchantments (Abundant Harvest, Carpet of Flowers) are ideal
- **Enchantment recursion** — Replenish, Open the Vaults, Hall of Heliod's Generosity to recover from hate
- **Aura tutors** (if aura-focused) — Totem Armor auras provide protection; Open the Armory, Ethereal Armor

---

## Anti-Synergy — Avoid

- Non-enchantment permanents that don't advance the enchantment count (dilutes the engine)
- Heavy instant/sorcery package — these don't trigger enchantress payoffs
- Auras on creatures vulnerable to removal — lose the enchantment when the creature dies; use Totem Armor auras to mitigate
