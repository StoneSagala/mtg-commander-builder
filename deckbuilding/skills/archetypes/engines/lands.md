# Engine: Lands Matter

Generate value from playing, having, or returning lands. The engine rewards land density with extra mana, damage, creature creation, or direct win conditions — making every land drop meaningful beyond just producing mana.

---

## How Value Is Generated

**Landfall** — triggers whenever a land enters; Omnath (multiple versions), Lotus Cobra, Tireless Tracker, Scute Swarm; stack multiple landfall triggers per land drop.

**Extra land drops** — playing additional lands per turn multiplies the engine; Azusa, Exploration, Teferi's Ageless Insight (draw more, play more).

**Land recursion** — returning lands from graveyard to hand/play enables repeated triggers; Crucible of Worlds, Ramunap Excavator, Life from the Loam.

**High land count payoffs** — Valakut, the Molten Pinnacle (damage per Mountain entering), Field of the Dead (Zombie per 7+ differently named lands).

---

## Core Card Types Required

- **Extra land drop effects (3–5)** — Exploration, Azusa Lost but Seeking, Dryad of the Ilysian Grove, Oracle of Mul Daya; the engine scales directly with extra drops
- **Land fetchers (4–6)** — Cultivate, Kodama's Reach, Sakura-Tribe Elder; both fix mana and trigger landfall twice (basic to hand + basic to play)
- **Landfall payoffs (3–5)** — at least 2 distinct payoff types so opponents can't answer just one
- **Land recursion (2–3)** — Crucible of Worlds, Ramunap Excavator; keeps the engine running when lands are destroyed or used as fetch fodder
- **Fetch lands** — each fetch land is 2 landfall triggers; Evolving Wilds, Terramorphic Expanse at budget; full fetches at higher brackets

## Anti-Synergy — Avoid

- Non-land ramp (rocks and dorks take up extra land drop slots)
- Low land count — Lands engine needs 40+ lands in the deck; running 37 starves the payoffs
- Mass land destruction in the same deck (Armageddon while relying on landfall is contradictory unless you have Crucible of Worlds)

## Common Pairings

- **Game Plans:** Go Wide (Scute Swarm, Field of the Dead), Combo (Dark Depths, Valakut loops), Control (land-based lock with Glacial Chasm)
- **Win Cons:** Life Depletion (combat with landfall creatures), Alternate Win Cons (Maze's End), Commander Damage (big landfall creatures)
