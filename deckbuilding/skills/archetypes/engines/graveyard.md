# Engine: Graveyard

Use the graveyard as a second hand. Fill it deliberately, then recur threats, generate value from cards in the yard, or reanimate finishers directly into play.

---

## How Value Is Generated

**Reanimation** — cheat large creatures into play at a fraction of their mana cost. Animate Dead (2 mana) + Elesh Norn, Grand Cenobite (7 mana) = massive tempo advantage.

**Recursion loops** — ETB creatures that return on death or with recursion spells create recurring value. Eternal Witness loops, Sun Titan chains.

**Graveyard as resource** — flashback (Past in Flames), escape (Kroxa), delve (Tasigur), and similar mechanics turn dead cards into live ones.

**Self-mill payoffs** — Stitcher's Supplier and Satyr Wayfinder fill the yard while advancing the plan.

---

## Core Card Types Required

- **Fill effects** — deliberate self-mill or discard to stock the graveyard (Entomb, Buried Alive, Faithless Looting, Frantic Search)
- **Reanimation spells** — Reanimate, Animate Dead, Necromancy, Unburied Alive; need 4+ at varying costs
- **Payoff targets** — the creatures worth reanimating; CMC 6+ with game-changing ETBs or static abilities
- **Graveyard protection** — Leyline of the Void in the sideboard isn't an option in Commander; need ways to fight graveyard hate (Kaya's Ghostform, Gaea's Blessing, redundancy through volume)

---

## Key Cards

Entomb · Buried Alive · Reanimate · Animate Dead · Necromancy · Unearth · Sun Titan · Eternal Witness · Past in Flames · Underworld Breach · Satyr Wayfinder · Stitcher's Supplier · Golgari Grave-Troll

---

## Anti-Synergy — Avoid

- Cards that exile from the graveyard on use (unless they're best-in-slot — e.g., Reanimate targets often have heavy life costs)
- Exile-based removal that removes your own targets
- Graveyard hate in your own deck (Tormod's Crypt)

---

## Graveyard Hate Counter-Plan (Required)

Opponents will run graveyard hate. Plan for it:
- **Volume** — mill more than hate can exile; run 15–20 fill effects
- **Instant-speed response** — reanimate in response to exile trigger (Necromancy at instant speed)
- **Backup lines** — a win condition that functions without the graveyard
