# Engine: Blink / ETB

Generate recurring value from enter-the-battlefield (ETB) effects by repeatedly returning creatures to hand or exile and replaying them. Each flicker resets the creature to its fresh state, retriggers its ETB, and generates another round of value.

---

## How Value Is Generated

**Flicker effects** — temporarily exile a creature then return it; Ephemerate, Conjurer's Closet, Teleportation Circle; the creature ETBs again. At instant speed, also dodges removal.

**ETB value creatures** — creatures whose ETB draws cards, removes permanents, creates tokens, or generates mana; the more powerful the ETB, the higher the flicker value.

**Bounce effects** — returning creatures to hand also resets ETBs, but costs mana to replay; blink is cleaner but bounce works at budget.

**Panharmonicon** — doubles all ETB triggers from artifacts and creatures; the single highest-value blink synergy card.

---

## Core Card Types Required

- **ETB value creatures (8–12)** — the payoffs; ETBs that draw cards, generate mana, create tokens, or remove threats; without powerful ETBs, flickering is irrelevant
- **Flicker effects (4–6)** — ideally at instant speed (Ephemerate, Ghostly Flicker, Conjurer's Closet); instant-speed flicker also protects from removal
- **Panharmonicon effects (1–3)** — Panharmonicon, Anointed Procession (token ETBs), Lithoform Engine; multipliers for the engine
- **ETB draw creatures specifically** — Cloudblazer, Mulldrifter, Baleful Strix; keeping hand full is essential when replaying creatures costs mana

## Anti-Synergy — Avoid

- Creatures with no ETB (vanilla creatures are dead weight)
- Auras (exile-and-return drops the aura)
- Equipment (remains on board, doesn't need to be re-equipped unless specifically triggered)
- "Until end of turn" effects — don't benefit from flickering since they expire anyway

---

## Bracket Scaling

- **Brackets 1–2:** Conjurer's Closet + value ETBs; Restoration Angel as interactive piece
- **Bracket 3:** Panharmonicon; Brago, King Eternal untap engine; Felidar Guardian + Saheeli Rai infinite (bracket check)
- **Brackets 4–5:** Felidar Guardian combo lines; Thassa, Deep-Dwelling + any ETB win; Eldrazi Displacer infinite with Phyrexian Altar

---

## Common Pairings

- **Game Plans:** Combo (infinite flicker loops), Control (instant-speed flicker as protection), Go Wide (token ETBs flickered repeatedly)
- **Win Cons:** Life Depletion (combat or drain ETBs), Alternate Win Cons (Thassa's Oracle with Felidar combo)
