# Don Andres, the Renegade — Commander
# Theme: Grixis theft-chaos — steal opponents' creatures and spells, buff them into deathtouch/menace monsters
# Bracket: 4 | Avg CMC: ~3.2 | Budget: ~$250
# Date: 2026-06-17

## Non-Negotiables
- **Coercive Recruiter**: The combo piece. With Don Andres out, stolen creatures become Pirates on ETB → triggers Recruiter → steal another → chains until the board is yours.
- **Confusion in the Ranks**: ETB chaos engine fueled by Don Andres's Treasure tokens — each Treasure entering swaps an opponent's artifact.
- **Admiral Beckett Brass**: When 3 pirates deal combat damage, permanently steal any non-land permanent. With Don Andres making everything a Pirate, this fires constantly.
- **Insurrection**: Alpha strike finisher — steal all creatures, swing for lethal.
- **Fierce Guardianship**: Free counter when Don Andres is in play. Protects key theft turns.
- **Demonic Tutor**: Find Coercive Recruiter, Confusion in the Ranks, or any missing piece.
- **Lightning Greaves**: Stolen creatures need haste or they can't attack. Also protects Don Andres from removal.
