# Teval, Arbiter of Virtue — Commander
# Theme: Drain to negative life, swap totals to kill; or set opponents to exactly 13 and let Triskaidekaphobia finish them
# Bracket: 4 | Avg CMC: ~3.1 | Budget: ~$250
# Date: 2026-07-04

## Non-Negotiables
- Triskaidekaphobia: Opponents lose 13 life at upkeep if at exactly 13 — primary kill line
- Repay in Kind: Set all players to your life total — if you're at 13, everyone hits 13 → Triskaidekaphobia kills
- Platinum Angel: You can't lose the game — allows going to negative life before swapping
- Lich's Mastery: You can't lose the game — redundant protection for negative-life kill line
- Mirror Universe: Exchange life totals with opponent — puts opponents at your low/negative life total
- Soul Conduit: Exchange life totals — second life swap effect
- Magus of the Mirror: Exchange life totals — third life swap effect, creature-based
- Necropotence: Pay life to draw cards — turns Teval's self-drain into card advantage
- Jerren, Corrupted Bishop: Bonus flip into Ormendahl when passing through 13 life on the way down
- Drift of Phantasms: Transmute for any 3-CMC card — finds Jerren or other 3-drops
- Perplex: Transmute for any 3-CMC card — second transmute, also doubles as counterspell
- Clutch of the Undercity: Transmute for any 3-CMC card — third transmute
