## Non-Negotiables
- **Massacre Wurm**: The kill. Three simultaneous ETBs = -6/-6 to all creatures, each death triggers 6 life loss. Primary win condition.
- **Buried Alive**: Puts Massacre Wurm + 2 others into GY in one card. Most efficient combo enabler in the deck.
- **Entomb**: Instant-speed GY tutor. Hold up mana all game, snap Massacre Wurm in at end of opponent's turn.
- **Rite of Replication**: Backup win con from brief. Kicked on Massacre Wurm = 5 copies, -10/-10 simultaneously, 10 life per creature death.
- **Teferi's Veil**: Creatures that attack phase out. Encore tokens must attack → phase out → miss the "sacrifice at beginning of next end step" trigger → kept permanently.
- **Teferi's Veil**: Creatures that attack become phased out. Encore tokens must attack → phase out → miss the "sacrifice at beginning of next end step" trigger → kept permanently.
- **Panharmonicon**: Doubles ETB triggers. Each encore Massacre Wurm token fires -2/-2 twice = -4/-4 per token. Three tokens = -12/-12 to everything.
