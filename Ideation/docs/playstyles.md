# Commander Playstyles

Reference for the ideation conversation. Use these descriptions to help players identify what style of gameplay they enjoy — not to gatekeep their choices.

---

## Engines (How the Deck Generates Resources)

### Enchantress
Builds around enchantments that reward playing more enchantments. Card draw is built into the strategy. Feels: snowballing, resilient, hard to interact with.

### Artifacts
Artifact synergies, ETBs, sacrifice loops, or storm. Ranges from value engines to combo kill. Feels: consistent, can win out of nowhere.

### Graveyard
Uses the graveyard as a resource — recursion, reanimation, dredge, flashback. Resilient to spot removal. Feels: inevitability, comes back from wipes.

### Aristocrats
Sac outlets + death triggers + payoffs. Wins by accumulating incremental value or assembling a combo loop. Feels: grindy, punishes sweepers by converting them into resources.

### Tribal
Cards that care about creature types. Builds around synergy between creatures of the same type. Feels: thematic, linear, can go wide quickly.

### Tokens
Creates lots of small creatures to go wide, overwhelm, or feed sacrifice engines. Feels: resilient, scales with the board.

### Counters
+1/+1 counters or charge counters as the primary resource. Can play aggressive or combo. Feels: snowballing, creatures get out of hand quickly.

### Spellslinger
Instants and sorceries as the primary fuel. Card draw, storm, or copy effects. Feels: explosive, tempo-driven.

### Lands
Lands are the engine — landfall triggers, land recursion, land-based combos. Feels: unique, hard to interact with, high ceiling.

### Blink/ETB
Flickering creatures to retrigger enter-the-battlefield effects. Value engine or infinite combo. Feels: toolboxy, interactive, flexible.

### Theft/Chaos
Taking or copying opponents' things, or introducing random elements. Feels: chaotic, situational, high-variance fun.

## Game Plans (How the Deck Wins)

| Game Plan | What It Does | Power Level Fit |
|-----------|-------------|-----------------|
| **Go-Wide** | Flood the board with creatures, win through combat | 2–4 |
| **Go-Tall** | Build one or a few enormous threats | 2–4 |
| **Combo** | Assemble 2–3 card infinite or near-infinite win condition | 3–5 |
| **Burn** | Deal direct damage to life totals outside combat | 2–4 |
| **Stax** | Lock opponents out of playing the game | 4–5 |
| **Control** | Counter and remove everything until you win | 3–5 |
| **Politics** | Deal-making, voting, group hug, incentivizing behavior | 1–3 |

---

## Win Conditions

| Win Con | Typical Setup |
|---------|--------------|
| **Life depletion** | Direct damage, drain triggers, combat over many turns |
| **Commander damage** | 21 combat damage from a single commander |
| **Mill** | Opponent draws from an empty library |
| **Poison/Infect** | 10 poison counters on an opponent |
| **Alternate win card** | Thassa's Oracle, Thopter/Sword, etc. |

## Matching Playstyle to a Player

Ask: *"What does a great game feel like to you?"*

- **"I want to have a ton of cards in hand"** → draw engines (enchantress, spellslinger, tribal)
- **"I want to feel unstoppable"** → graveyard, artifacts, counters
- **"I want to be the one making chaos"** → theft/chaos, politics
- **"I just want to attack and end games"** → go-wide, go-tall, burn
- **"I like executing a plan perfectly"** → combo, stax, control
- **"I want lands to matter"** → lands engine
