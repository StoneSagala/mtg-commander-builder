# Commander Brackets

The official Commander bracket system (1-5) introduced by Wizards of the Coast in February 2025. Source: EDHREC / mtgcommander.net

---

## Bracket 1 — Exhibition
**Ultra-casual.** Self expression and shared experience over winning.

**Restrictions:**
- No cards from the Game Changer list
- No intentional 2-card infinite combos
- No mass land denial
- No extra turn cards
- Tutors should be sparse

**Who plays here:** Brand new players, pure kitchen table, budget brews.

---

## Bracket 2 — Core
**Average precon level.** The easiest reference point is the current average preconstructed deck.

**Restrictions:**
- No cards from the Game Changer list
- No intentional 2-card infinite combos
- No mass land denial
- Extra turn cards allowed in low quantities — not intended to be chained or looped
- Tutors should be sparse

**Who plays here:** Most kitchen table pods, FNM Commander, precon players.

---

## Bracket 3 — Upgraded
**Beyond precon strength.** Souped up and ready to play. The most common bracket for experienced casual players.

**Restrictions:**
- Up to 3 cards from the Game Changer list
- No intentional **early game** 2-card infinite combos (late game combos allowed)
- No mass land denial
- Extra turn cards allowed in low quantities — not intended to be chained or looped

**Who plays here:** Players who have significantly upgraded precons, experienced players who want power without going full competitive. Majority of decks live between bracket 2 and 3.

---

## Bracket 4 — Optimized
**High power. Time to go wild.** Highly synergistic decks built to win with the best cards available.

**Restrictions:**
- No restrictions (other than the banned list)

**Notes:** All Game Changers on the table. Tutors, fast mana, infinite combos — all fair game. Social contract still exists but power is prioritized.

**Who plays here:** Players who want to bring the best version of their deck, high-power casual, competitive-adjacent pods.

---

## Bracket 5 — cEDH
**Fully competitive.** High power with a metagame-focused mindset. Winning matters more than self expression.

**Restrictions:**
- No restrictions (other than the banned list)

**Who plays here:** Tournament Commander, dedicated competitive players, cEDH Discord communities.

## Bracket Matching Guide

| Question | Likely Bracket |
|----------|---------------|
| "We play unmodified precons" | 1-2 |
| "We play for fun, games go long" | 2 |
| "We have synergistic decks but avoid combos" | 2-3 |
| "Some people have combos in our pod" | 3 |
| "We run tutors and game changers" | 3-4 |
| "We try to win as fast as possible" | 4-5 |
| "We play on competitive Discord servers" | 5 |

---

## Common Mismatches to Flag

- **Wants combos, says bracket 1-2:** No infinite combos until bracket 3
- **Wants mass land denial:** Not allowed until bracket 4
- **Wants to chain extra turns:** Not allowed at any bracket — low quantities only up to bracket 3, unrestricted at 4-5
- **Wants 4+ Game Changers, says bracket 3:** Bracket 3 caps at 3 Game Changers — needs to move to bracket 4
- **Budget under $100, wants bracket 5:** cEDH fast mana (Mox Diamond, Mana Vault, etc.) is expensive — bracket 3-4 is realistic
